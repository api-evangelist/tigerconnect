Around half of U.S. physicians report burnout, driven by administrative overload, uneven call burden, and little control over their own schedules. Discover the five capabilities health systems should look for in modern physician scheduling software — intelligent automation, a centralized source of truth, integrated clinical communication, workload and fatigue reporting, and mobile real-time schedule management — and how each addresses a root cause of burnout while improving access, coverage, and operational efficiency at enterprise scale.
