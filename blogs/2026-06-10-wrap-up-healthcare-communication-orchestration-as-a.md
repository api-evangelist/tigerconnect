Healthcare communication orchestration is emerging as a strategic digital pillar for health systems, helping care teams move beyond fragmented alerts, manual coordination, and disconnected workflows. This wrap-up explores how orchestrated communication can reduce cognitive burden, improve throughput, strengthen care team efficiency, and unlock measurable ROI while supporting better experiences for patients and the clinicians who care for them.
